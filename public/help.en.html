Quick topics

  • Creating points — click on the canvas.
  • Drawing segments — select Segment tool and click two points.
  • Compass — select a segment or two points to copy their length, then choose an existing point or click to place the circle center.
  • Bisector tool — choose three points (the second is the vertex), two arms or an existing angle. No drawn arms are required for point inputs.
  • Copy — select objects in multiselect mode, click Copy, then Paste. Copies retain their own construction inputs. Click a solid or frame its complete group to select connected 3D solids together. Their attached constructions, angles and sections are copied with them. Only the pasted copy is selected and ready to move.
  • Prism — select a base polygon and choose the tool, or enter the number of sides and click the canvas. Four sides create a cube. Drag the selected top base or its vertical handle to change height. Dragging the lower base moves the whole solid.
  • Pyramid — select a base polygon and choose the tool, or enter the number of sides and click the canvas. Drag the selected apex to change height and tilt according to the locks. Its vertical handle changes height alone.
  • Section plane — choose three distinct, non-collinear points of one solid or a connected 3D group. Choose existing vertices or points, or click edges to create points on them. The app draws the solid’s cross-section polygon in the plane through those points. The section updates when the points or solid change. Set its color and fill in the Style menu.
